# Vision

A lightweight app to track emotions throughout a day.

Tracking emotions throughout the day can be useful because emotions are often data, not just feelings.
When you notice patterns over time, you can better understand what affects your energy, focus, motivation, relationships, and well-being.

This is mainly needed for Better self-awareness and Connecting emotions to behavior. 
This should not turn emotions into "performance metrics".

Core app principles:
- offline first
- minimal UI
- easy to use
- mobile friendly
