# Vision

A lightweight app to track emotions throughout the day with minimal friction.

## Mission
Enable people to capture how they feel in a simple way and use that awareness to make better daily decisions.

## Philosophy
- Emotions are valid signals, not performance metrics.
- Capture feelings quickly and unobtrusively.
- Keep the UI simple and friendly so tracking becomes a habit.
- Prioritize privacy by keeping all data local and offline-first.

## Target users
- people who want greater self-awareness
- users who prefer lightweight tools over complex mood journals
- people who need offline support and local storage
- Android mobile users who want fast, low-overhead apps

## Value proposition
- fast emotion logging with a single tap
- easy review of emotional patterns over time
- consistent daily awareness without distraction

## Long-term goals
- become the simplest daily mood tracker for mobile users
- support basic charts and history review
- offer safe local backup and optional sync later
- keep app size small and the experience uncluttered

## Core principles
- offline first
- minimal UI
- easy to use
- mobile friendly
- privacy-focused
